Reply speed

Where the wait actually is — measured, not guessed, on 24 Nov 2026.

~0.5s
Telegram delivering your message (webhook test)
5s
Current check interval — the fastest polling allowed
10–20s
Muse waking, reading & replying — the real bottleneck
Keep the 5-second check
Your current setup, with a 15-minute backup check. Reliable, no public link needed.
On
Fast answers for simple commands — not on yet
The one real shortcut available: /spaces, /review, /defaults and /help could be answered by the bot from saved answers, skipping the 10–20s wake-up. It isn't set up yet — flip this to ask Muse to set it up. Until then, every command waits for the 5-second check plus the wake-up. Full questions would still wake Muse.
Webhook push instead of polling
Tested: saves only ~2–3s of detection, needs a public tunnel that must stay running, and Telegram drops queued messages if it dies. Not recommended.

Honest bottom line: no route makes a full Muse reply truly instant, because the thinking itself takes the time. The fast-command switch above is the one change that could cut the wait for the commands you use most — it isn't on yet, and turning it on here sends the request to Muse in the chat to set up.
