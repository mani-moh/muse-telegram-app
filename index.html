How this works

This screen is Muse’s front door inside Telegram, not the Muse app itself. Every tap sends its command to the bot and Muse replies in this chat — so anything Muse can do in the app can be started from here.

How fast?
Telegram delivers in under a second; Muse wakes and replies in about 10–20 seconds. Nothing here can make a full reply instant.
Approvals
Buying, paying, sending and deleting always need your tap on the approval card in the Muse app. This screen can only show what’s waiting — tap Review to see it.
Settings
Settings live in the Muse app, not here. Tap below and Muse will give you the exact path.
